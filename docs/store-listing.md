## Description

From “what should we do?” to “we’re going” in under a minute.

Tell Whimway the mood, the budget and who’s coming. It builds a night of two to four
real places, with times, travel between stops and what it should cost. Not a list of
ideas: a plan you can follow.

**Say it in a sentence.** “Saturday night with my girlfriend, $100 total, romantic but
a little adventurous.” Whimway works out who, when, budget and how you’re getting around,
and shows it back so you can change anything.

**See the whole night.** A timeline of stops with opening-hours checks, price ranges,
and the walk, transit ride or drive in between.

**Change it in plain words.** “Make it cheaper.” “Swap dinner for something more fun.”
“It’s raining, keep it indoors.” Only the parts that need to change, change.

**Book and go.** Find a table, get tickets, open directions or call a ride from each stop.

**Share it.** Send the plan as a link. Friends can open it without the app and make their own copy.

**It learns your taste.** Quick polls and a thumbs up or down after each night out
shape what you see next.

Whimway is launching in the Greater Toronto Area: Toronto, Vaughan, Richmond Hill and Markham.

**Whimway Plus** removes the monthly plan limit and adds planning around your saved
places, pace and alcohol-free options, and one-tap surprise plans. Free includes five
plans a month.

Prices and opening hours come from published sources and can change. Confirm with the venue before you go.

## Privacy answers (App Store "App Privacy", Play "Data safety")

- **Location:** approximate, only when the user taps "Near me"; used for app functionality; not stored on the server.
- **Identifiers:** a random per-install id for usage counts and anonymous votes; not linked to identity unless signed in.
- **Usage data:** product interactions, only with consent.
- **Contact info:** email address, only if the user signs in.
- **Not collected:** contacts, photos, health, financial details, browsing history. No advertising or tracking.
- Account deletion is available in the app (You → Delete my account).

## Review notes for Apple and Google

- No sign-in is required to use the app.
- Subscriptions: Whimway Plus monthly and yearly, with a 7-day free trial. Restore purchases is on the Plus screen.
- External links (reservations, tickets, rides, maps) open the venue or a third-party service; Whimway takes no payment for them.
