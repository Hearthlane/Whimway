A few ground rules.

Whimway is a beta area-discovery and outing-planning application. Place information comes from external providers. Recommendations are suggestions and do not guarantee admission, safety, suitability, current business hours or accessibility.

Check before you go.

Confirm opening hours, prices, reservations, dietary needs and access with each venue. Published price ranges and category browsing estimates may omit taxes, tips, ticketed events, transport and purchases. Route information may omit sidewalks, traffic or temporary closures.

Fair use.

Usage allowances protect service availability. Do not automate excessive requests, abuse public sharing, upload unlawful content, or attempt to access another person’s private plans. Sharing is an explicit choice; share only information you intend others to see.

Before public launch.

The operator must obtain legal review and complete identity, contact, jurisdiction, acceptable-use, liability, dispute-resolution, privacy and provider-policy provisions. Whimway is a working name; domain and trademark clearance remain outstanding.
