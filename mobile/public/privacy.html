Privacy, thoughtfully.

Whimway sends your selected exploration area, outing request and constraints to Google Maps Platform and Anthropic (Claude) to retrieve places and design your outing. Review those providers’ privacy policies before using their services.

Your choices stay yours.

Guest plans and optional preferences stay in this browser until you delete them. Device plans store Place IDs, visit order, duration and your choices; venue details and photographs are refreshed. Cloud plans and preferences are stored in Supabase only when you sign in and choose to save them.

Location and sharing.

Browser location is requested only after you choose Use my location. Share links make stop IDs, visit times, plan titles and a map area derived from public stops public to anyone with the link. Private prompts, dietary and accessibility requirements and personal route anchors are removed. You may revoke links; deleting a cloud plan removes its links.

In the Whimway app.

The app keeps your taste profile, plans and feedback on your device. “Near me” asks for your location once, rounds it to about 100 metres and uses it only as the planning area. Usage statistics (for example “plan saved”) are sent only if you allow them, with a random install ID and never your prompts, places or location.

Polls, reactions and community signals.

When you answer a poll, tap 👍/👎 on a plan or stop, or rate an outing, an anonymous vote (a random install ID, a venue type or place ID, and up or down) is stored to learn what people enjoy. Results are only used in aggregate, a venue or category only counts once at least five different people have reacted, and nobody can read individual votes. Re-voting replaces your earlier vote.

Usage and deletion.

Server logs record event names and timestamps, without prompts or precise locations. Rate limiting stores hashed network identifiers for up to 24 hours. No advertising analytics are installed. Delete device data, preferences, plans or your account from Profile and Saved. Contact details, controller identity, retention schedules, international transfer terms and jurisdiction-specific rights must be completed by the operator before launch.
